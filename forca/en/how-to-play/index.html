How to play Forca

Guess the secret word, one letter at a time, before the hangman drawing is complete. "Forca" is Portuguese for hangman. Here are the rules, how levels and hints work, and a few strategies to guess more words.

Play in your browser

Free, with no sign-up. It works on phones, tablets and computers. The Android version, which also runs on Google TV, is coming soon to Google Play.

The goal

Find the secret word before you miss 6 letters. Each wrong letter draws one more part of the figure on the gallows. When the figure is complete, the round is over.

A round, step by step

  1. On the home screen, pick a category, or "All categories", and tap Play.
  2. The word shows up as a row of blanks, one for each letter. Hyphens and spaces, as in DRAGON FRUIT, are shown from the start.
  3. Pick a letter on the on-screen keyboard. On a computer, you can also type the letter on your keyboard.
  4. If the letter is in the word, it shows up in every position. If it isn't, it goes to the list of wrong letters, and the figure gets one more part.
  5. You win when you complete the word, and lose on the sixth miss. After a win, the Next level button starts a round at the next level. After a loss, the game shows the word, and the Play again button starts another round at the same level.

Eight categories

There are about 700 words in English and another 700 in Portuguese, in eight categories:

  • Animals, such as hummingbird and kangaroo
  • Fruits, such as dragon fruit and passion fruit
  • Countries
  • Professions
  • Objects
  • Sports
  • Food
  • Nature

Pick one category or play them all mixed together.

Accents

You don't need to worry about accents, which matter in the Portuguese words. The letter A counts for A, Á, Â, Ã and À, the letter C counts for C and Ç, and so on. The word shows each letter with its accent.

Levels

You start at level 1 and go up one level with each win. A loss never takes a level away. At higher levels, the words have less common letters, which are harder to guess.

Hints

A hint reveals one of the hidden letters, the hardest one to guess, and doesn't count as a miss. On a computer, the ? key also asks for a hint.

  • You start with 5 hints.
  • In the browser and on Google TV, hints come back to 5 once a day.
  • On Android phones and tablets, when you run out, you can watch a short ad to get 5 more. Watching is always optional.

English and Portuguese

The game uses your device's language, and you can switch it in Settings. Each language has its own words.

Statistics

The home screen shows your wins, your losses, your current winning streak and your best streak. They are kept on your device.

Strategies

  • Start with the vowels. E and A show up in most English words.
  • Then try common consonants, such as T, N, S and R.
  • Use the category: it helps you picture the words that fit the blanks.
  • Look at the length of the word and the letters already shown before you try a rare letter.
  • When you're stuck, take a hint: it reveals the hardest letter.
